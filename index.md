## About Me
I am a **PhD Candidate in Computer Science** at Stanford University, advised by Prof. Alan Turing. My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing, multimodal machine learning, and responsible AI for scientific applications.

I completed my BSc in Computer Science + Mathematics at MIT (summa cum laude) in 2022, where I was awarded the MIT Presidential Fellowship and the ACM Undergraduate Research Award. Prior to starting my PhD, I worked as a Research Internship at **OpenAI** (2022) and **Google DeepMind** (2023).

## Research Interests
- Large Language Model (LLM) safety & alignment
- Multimodal foundation models for computational biology
- Bias mitigation and responsible AI in vision-language systems
- Parameter-efficient fine-tuning for domain-specific models
